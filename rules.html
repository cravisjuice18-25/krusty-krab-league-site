League Format

Eight teams. Redraft. PPR scoring. Nine starting players.

Regular Season

The regular season runs for 14 weeks. Teams are ranked by record, with points for as the tiebreaker.

Road to the Championship

  1. The top three seeds qualify directly for the playoffs.
  2. Seeds four and five play in Week 15 for the final playoff spot.
  3. The playoffs take place in Weeks 16 and 17.
